FocusFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

FocusFlow is a student planning and study management web application designed to help students organise academic tasks, deadlines, schedules and study sessions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FocusFlow may collect, how it is used, and how Google Calendar integration works.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a FocusFlow account, the application may collect your name, email address and password.

Passwords are processed using password hashing before they are stored. FocusFlow does not intentionally store your password in plain text.

FocusFlow may also store information that you enter into the application, including study tasks, deadlines, schedules and study session information.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by FocusFlow is used to provide the main features of the application, including account access, task management, schedule management and study-session recording.

The information is also used to allow the application to display your entered planning and study information while you use FocusFlow.

3. Google Calendar

FocusFlow provides an optional Google Calendar integration. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, FocusFlow requests permission to create calendar events in your Google Calendar.

FocusFlow uses the Google Calendar API to create calendar events based on schedules that you choose to add to Google Calendar.

FocusFlow does not use Google Calendar access for advertising or selling personal information.

Google Calendar access is optional. You can continue using the main FocusFlow planning features without connecting Google Calendar.

4. Data Sharing

FocusFlow does not sell your personal information.

Information submitted to FocusFlow is used to operate and provide the application's features. Information may be processed by the services used to host and operate the application.

5. Data Security

FocusFlow uses reasonable technical measures to protect information used by the application. Account passwords are hashed before being stored.

However, no internet-based application can guarantee complete security of information transmitted or stored online.

6. Google Services

When you use Google Calendar integration, Google's own privacy policies and terms also apply to your use of Google services.

You can review Google's privacy information at: Google Privacy Policy

7. Your Choices

Google Calendar integration is optional. You can choose whether or not to grant FocusFlow access to your Google Calendar.

You should only provide information that is necessary for using the application's features.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or operation of FocusFlow change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
